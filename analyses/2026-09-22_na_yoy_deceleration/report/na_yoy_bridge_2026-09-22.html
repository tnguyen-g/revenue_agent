The answer

  • Mostly an LY base effect. Both years ran an ILS-stack Monday and an order-discount Tuesday, but LY's OD Tuesday was ~2.2× bigger (~$1.07M vs ~$0.49M GB on OD campaigns), and LY's Monday was weak (−7.1% orders vs the previous Monday). The LY base effect is -526 bps of the -811 bps M1VFM DtD (UNAGI basis; UE shows -806 bps).
  • TY Tuesday was not weak against its own last week: M1VFM +1.0% and orders +1.5% vs Tue 9/15. Against the normal Mon→Tue pattern TY looks -214 bps weaker, but part of that is a strong TY Monday (touch orders +16.9% vs the previous Monday).
  • Where TY does look soft (funnel): conversion (orders/UDV) on touch and app Direct, Non-Brand SEM impressions on app and touch (a Mon→Tue drop that has recurred three weeks running), and app push impressions. Higher AOV (Travel) offsets most of it.
  • Not the driver: JPROD-969 (Orders Index API 404s, 11:42–15:10 UTC) is only ~1.4pp worse than the hours after it.
